PAMS Interval Checker local verification
2026-11-26

RTL simulation
Icarus Verilog 12.0 and cocotb 2.1.0 passed all three tests.
The compression test checked 12,480 transactions across all 3,120 code
intervals for d=4, 5, 10, and 11. Each interval was tested at its first and
last coefficient and at the adjacent coefficients outside the interval.
The share split varied with a fixed seed.

The first two tests also checked reset during division, deselection,
ready/valid backpressure, status retention, and malformed share and
ciphertext fields. See rtl-results.xml.

The three results in order are MATCH, mismatch, and ERROR.

GF180 physical build
LibreLane 3.0.14 and the GF180MCU D PDK revision e8daeda73ca8f5814dbc0b11d1d05802251a3750
produced a routed 346.64 by 160.72 micrometer tile. The standard-cell area
was 20,957.6 square micrometers at 40.3% reported utilization.
Worst setup and hold slack at the 100 ns clock period were +47.24 ns and
+0.48 ns, respectively. Detailed routing, Magic DRC, and LVS reported
zero errors. OpenROAD reported up to 121 slew violations and one
capacitance violation across the analyzed corners. See layout-metrics.csv.
All 12 Tiny Tapeout precheck checks passed with KLayout 0.30.8 and the
same PDK revision. See precheck-summary.md and precheck-results.xml.

The powered gate-level netlist passed all three tests with the GF180
simulation models, including 64 sampled boundary transactions and the
handshake and malformed-packet checks. See gate-level-results.xml.

gf180-layout.png and gf180-layout.svg were rendered from pams-gf180.gds
with the Tiny Tapeout renderer. The LEF and powered Verilog are included
for inspection and simulation.

FPGA compile
pams-fabricfox-v2.bin targets the Tiny Tapeout FabricFox v2 iCE40UP5K
ASIC simulator breakout. Yosys, nextpnr and IceStorm generated it using
the pin constraints from Tiny Tapeout support tools commit 21568ef.
The routed design uses 260 of 5,280 logic cells and met its 12 MHz
compile constraint. nextpnr estimated a 37.39 MHz maximum clock.
Programming and testing a board remain pending.
